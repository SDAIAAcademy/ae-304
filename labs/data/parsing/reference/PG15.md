Undergraduate Study and Examinations Regulation
Chapter 4: Withdrawal and postponement
Article 26: Postponement of study
A student may postpone study before the semester begins for no more than two consecutive semesters and no more than 3 semesters in total during the programme. A postponed semester is not counted in the maximum study duration. Students should keep their contact details in the portal up to date. The college council reviews the results of each semester and reports to the university council.
This regulation is effective from 2022-09-01.
