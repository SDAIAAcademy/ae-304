Undergraduate Study and Examinations Regulation
Chapter 3: Study load and attendance
Article 17: Attendance and denial of the final examination
A student must attend the lectures and practical sessions of every course. A student whose absences without an accepted excuse exceed 25% of the contact hours of a course is denied entry to its final examination and receives the grade DN (denied) in that course. The instructor announces the absence record in the student portal every two weeks. The college council reviews the results of each semester and reports to the university council. The academic advisor reviews the study plan with the student every semester.
This regulation is effective from 2022-09-01.
