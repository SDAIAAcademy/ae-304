Undergraduate Study and Examinations Regulation
Chapter 6: Grading and the grade point average
Article 41: The grading scale
Course grades are recorded on a five-point scale: A+ (95 to 100) is 5.00, A (90 to 94) is 4.75, B+ (85 to 89) is 4.50, B (80 to 84) is 4.00, C+ (75 to 79) is 3.50, C (70 to 74) is 3.00, D+ (65 to 69) is 2.50, D (60 to 64) is 2.00 and F (below 60) is 1.00. The university publishes its regulations in Arabic and English; the Arabic text prevails in case of difference. Dates in the academic calendar are announced at the start of each semester.
This regulation is effective from 2022-09-01.
