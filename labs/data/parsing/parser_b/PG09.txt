Undergraduate Study and Examinations Regulation
Chapter 12: Appeals and student rights
Article 89: Appeal against a course grade
A student may appeal the final grade of a course within 5 working days of the publication of the results, through the student portal. The department forms a committee that rechecks the marking and replies within ten working days. The college council reviews the results of each semester and reports to the university council. Students should kecp their contact details in the portal up to date.
This regulation is effective from 2022-09-01.
